    The Open + Online program offers FREE website building, logo & branding design, or expert SEO
    (search engine optimization) consulting.

    HOW OUR WEBSITE PROJECTS WORK

    PROJECT GUIDANCE

    A dedicated Project Manager will collect your materials and guide you through the program

    DESIGN SESSIONS

    You'll have two sessions with your web designer to plan out and review your custom website

    VIDEO TUTORIAL

    We'll set you up with a walkthrough video of your new site, showing you how to manage it

    SPECIALIST SUPPORT

    A Tech Speicalist will help you launch your site, and can assist you with advice & guidance going forward

    IS IT REALLY FREE?

    • We pay for your first full year of website hosting using the Squarespace.com builder & platform

    • After building, we can help register a domain name for your new website, if you don't own one already

    • Professional designers typically charge thousands for their services. We've got the design fees covered for you!

    • For logo & branding projects, all designs are done from scratch and you'll have full ownership of the final files

    • For SEO improvements, hiring a consultant can be expensive, but learning from our expert is absolutely FREE!
